What is Rice Bran Oil?

Rice bran oil is a healthy edible oil extracted from the outer bran layer of rice grains. It has become increasingly popular because of its balanced fat composition, mild flavor, and high smoke point. People searching for what is rice bran oil often want to know whether it is suitable for cooking, frying, and daily food preparation.

This oil contains antioxidants, vitamin E compounds, and oryzanol, which may support heart health and cholesterol management. Due to these nutritional properties, rice bran oil is widely used in homes, restaurants, and food industries.

Rice Bran Oil Benefits
